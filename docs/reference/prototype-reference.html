Non-EU groups

Outside the EU? Three questions

A non-EU group can be caught twice: via a large EU subsidiary (FY2027) and at group level, Article 40a (FY2028).

Answer in order. Stop as soon as one answer says it does not apply to you.

  1. 1
    Route 1 · EU subsidiary · from FY2027

    Do you have an EU subsidiary with over 1,000 employees and over €450M turnover?

    Look at the subsidiary on its own (consolidated) figures. An EU sub-group parent counts too.

    YesThat subsidiary reports like any EU company, from FY2027. Keep going: Route 2 can apply on top.
    NoRoute 1 does not apply. Go to question 2.
    The fine print

    It can be exempt if the non-EU parent voluntarily publishes a consolidated report under the full ESRS (or an equivalent standard) that covers it.

  2. 2
    Route 2 · Article 40a · from FY2028

    Did your group earn over €450M in the EU, two years in a row?

    Add up the net turnover of everything your group sells in the EU.

    YesGo to question 3.
    NoArticle 40a does not apply. You are done here.
  3. 3
    Route 2 · Article 40a · from FY2028

    Do you have an EU subsidiary or branch with over €200M turnover?

    There is no headcount criterion for this one.

    YesThat entity reports for the whole group, from FY2028 (published in 2029).
    NoArticle 40a does not apply.
    Good to know

    Dedicated standards for non-EU groups are expected from October 2027 at the earliest. Financial holding undertakings can opt out.

Which standard

Which standards for your 2026 report? You choose

For FY2026 the rules let you decide how to report. Compare the three routes, pick the one that fits your team and your auditor, and say in your report which you used.

Good to know Pre-Omnibus ESRS are the 2023 standards you used for FY2025 (as amended in 2025). Revised ESRS are the new, slimmer ones. From FY2027 the revised ESRS are mandatory for everyone in scope.

Our tip Starting with ESRS now? Route C is usually the natural fit: you have no 2025 report to compare with, so you can begin on the slimmer standards. Whichever route you choose, agree it with your auditor early.

Where this comes from

All three routes come from one legal text: Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563 (Official Journal, 21 September 2026, in force from 10 November 2026). Article 2 sets the choice for FY2026.

  • Route A and C Article 2(1)(a): report FY2026 under the Pre-Omnibus ESRS (A) or the Revised ESRS (C).
  • Route B Article 2(1)(b): Pre-Omnibus ESRS plus up to eight reliefs, taken from ESRS 1 paragraphs 27, 32-33, 74-75, 90, 91, 92, 106 and 110.
  • All routes Article 2(2): state in your report which version you applied. Article 3: the Revised ESRS apply to all in scope from financial years starting on or after 1 January 2027.
