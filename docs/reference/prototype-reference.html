Who reports when

Do you have to report? Two quick tests

Short answer: EU companies report from FY2027 if they pass both tests. Non-EU groups follow from FY2028.

EU companies, groups and EU-listed issuers
> 1,000 employeesAND> €450M net turnover

Over €450M net turnover and over 1,000 employees on average. First report: FY2027, published 2028.

For you Over both lines? You report from FY2027. Parents: use group figures.

Before: 250 employees and €50M turnover (any two of three criteria).

Non-EU groups (Article 40a)
> €450M EU turnover, 2 yearsANDEU subsidiary or branch > €200M

No headcount test. First report on FY2028, published in 2029.

For you Over both lines? Your EU entity reports for the group from FY2028.

Before: €150M EU turnover and a €40M branch or large subsidiary.

Non-EU groups

Outside the EU? Two ways in

A non-EU group can be caught twice: via a large EU subsidiary (FY2027) and at group level, Article 40a (FY2028).

Route 1 · from FY2027

A large EU subsidiary

For you If your EU subsidiary has over 1,000 employees and over €450M turnover, it reports like any EU company.

The fine print

This applies to any EU subsidiary, or EU sub-group parent, on its own (consolidated) figures. It can be exempt if the non-EU parent voluntarily publishes a consolidated report under the full ESRS (or an equivalent standard) that covers it.

Route 2 · from FY2028

Article 40a group report

For you If your group earns over €450M in the EU and you have an EU entity over €200M, that entity reports for the whole group.

The fine print

The €450M must be exceeded in two consecutive years, and the entity is an EU subsidiary or branch. There is no headcount test.

1. Add up your EU turnover

Over €450M, two years in a row?

2. Find your anchor entity

An EU subsidiary or branch over €200M? If not, Article 40a does not apply.

3. Plan your first report

It covers FY2028.

Good to know

Dedicated standards for non-EU groups are expected from October 2027 at the earliest. Financial holding undertakings can opt out.
